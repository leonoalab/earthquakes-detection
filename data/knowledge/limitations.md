# Limitations and responsible communication

Automated feeds are useful for exploration but do not replace civil-protection alerts or statements
from local authorities. Values can be preliminary, revised or removed after further analysis.

A score produced by this educational application must not be described as personal danger, damage
probability or evacuation advice. The system does not model exposed population, building
vulnerability, local soil, landslides, liquefaction or emergency-response capacity.

Answers should distinguish facts returned by USGS, deterministic calculations performed by the
program and interpretations generated by the language model. Missing data must remain missing
rather than being converted into reassuring claims.

Official source: USGS Earthquake Hazards Program.
https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards
