# Seismic hazard, risk and prediction

Seismic hazard describes the possibility of experiencing specified levels of ground motion in a
place and period. Risk combines hazard with exposed people and assets and their vulnerability. A
location can have high hazard but low exposure, or moderate hazard with important potential
consequences because many vulnerable assets are present.

A deterministic prediction would specify time, place and magnitude in advance with useful
precision. It must not be confused with a probabilistic forecast, an aftershock estimate or an
early warning issued after rupture begins. A cluster of recent events is not evidence that a larger
earthquake is about to happen.

Official source: USGS, Learn About Earthquake Hazards.
https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/science/learn-about-earthquake-hazards
