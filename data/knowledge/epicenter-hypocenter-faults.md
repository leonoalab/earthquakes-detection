# Epicenter, hypocenter and faults

The hypocenter, also called the focus, is the estimated point inside the Earth where rupture starts.
The epicenter is the point on the surface directly above it. A feed coordinate is an estimated
location, not a complete description of the part of the fault that ruptured.

A fault is a fracture or zone of fractures along which blocks of rock move. Large earthquakes can
rupture an extended area, so distance from the epicenter alone is not always the best description
of distance from the rupture.

Official source: USGS Earthquake Glossary.
