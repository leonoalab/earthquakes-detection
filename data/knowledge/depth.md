# Earthquake depth

Depth is the estimated vertical distance from the surface to the earthquake hypocenter. Shallow
events often produce stronger shaking near the source than deeper events of similar magnitude, but
depth alone does not determine damage or risk.

Depth estimates can change as more observations are processed. Values near common defaults should
not be presented as exact physical measurements without consulting the detailed event products and
their uncertainty.

Official source: USGS Earthquake Glossary.
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/education/glossary.php
