# Earthquake early warning

Earthquake early-warning systems detect an earthquake after rupture has begun. They use the first
arriving seismic waves to estimate the event and send a warning before the strongest shaking
reaches locations farther away. This is not earthquake prediction: the event is already underway.

Warning time depends on the distance from the rupture, processing and communication latency, and
the speed at which seismic waves travel. Locations close to the epicenter may receive little or no
warning. An alert also does not predict the exact shaking at every building.

Official source: USGS ShakeAlert.
https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/science/shakealert
