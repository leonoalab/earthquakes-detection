# The difference between magnitude and intensity

Magnitude estimates the size of an earthquake at its source. Intensity describes observed effects
at a particular location: how strongly the ground moved and what effects people and structures
experienced. One earthquake has a magnitude estimate but can produce many intensity values across
different places.

Distance, local geology and construction influence the observed effects. Did You Feel It? reports
can complement instruments, but the number of reports is not itself an intensity or damage value.

Official source: USGS Modified Mercalli Intensity Scale.
https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/modified-mercalli-intensity-scale
