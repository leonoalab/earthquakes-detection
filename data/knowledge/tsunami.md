# Earthquakes and tsunamis

Not every earthquake generates a tsunami. Generation depends on factors including an underwater
location, depth, magnitude and displacement of the seafloor. Magnitude alone cannot confirm that a
tsunami occurred or estimate its effects.

The `tsunami` field in a USGS summary record is a processing indicator associated with the event.
It is not an evacuation order and does not automatically confirm a dangerous wave. Safety decisions
must rely on official tsunami-warning centers and local authorities.

Official sources: NOAA Tsunami Warning Centers and USGS Earthquake Hazards Program.
https://www.tsunami.gov/
